The main tables judge responses truncated at 96 tokens, which could mostly measure the onset of compliance. We regenerated the undefended and always-on responses of seven framings for all 241 harmful test prompts at 256 tokens and re-judged them. Attack success barely moves. Without defense it is 88.8\% (96 tokens) versus 89.2\% (256) on fiction, 75.9\% versus 74.7\% on prefix injection, and 95.4\% versus 94.2\% on payload splitting. With the always-on steer it is 28.6\% versus 29.9\%, 4.6\% versus 5.0\%, and 0.4\% versus 0.8\%. The gap between steered and undefended responses, which drives every composed ASR in the main text, therefore does not depend on the truncation length.
